# Personas

## Persona 1: Operations Analyst

### Goal

Find reliable answers in internal documents without depending on colleagues or manual folder searches.

### Pain Points

- does not know where information is stored;
- loses time searching documents;
- receives inconsistent answers;
- depends on senior employees.

### Example Questions

- What is the reimbursement policy for business trips?
- What is the procedure for customer cancellation?
- Which document explains the onboarding process?

## Persona 2: Business Manager

### Goal

Ask questions about business data without writing SQL or waiting for BI reports.

### Pain Points

- depends on analysts to extract simple information;
- waits for reports;
- cannot explore data quickly;
- struggles to connect operational questions with structured data.

### Example Questions

- Which customers generated the highest revenue last month?
- Which region had the largest drop in sales?
- What are the top overdue invoices?

## Persona 3: Data or IT Team

### Goal

Provide a controlled AI layer over documents and databases.

### Pain Points

- uncontrolled AI usage creates security risks;
- generated SQL can be dangerous;
- model providers can create vendor lock-in;
- answers need traceability;
- LLM calls need logging and monitoring.

### Example Needs

- use local models first;
- audit questions and answers;
- restrict SQL access;
- return answer sources;
- change LLM providers without rewriting the application.
